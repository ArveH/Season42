Season42

An iPhone app for keeping track of which series and movies you watch across streaming services.

Everything the app knows is yours: entered by hand, stored on the device, and never sent anywhere. There is no account, no sync and no analytics. Searching for a series, a movie or a streaming service's logo is the one thing that needs a network; everything you have already added works with no connection at all.

The two pages this site exists for:

  • Privacy policy — what the app does and does not do with information.
  • Support — how to report a problem, and what support is for a one-person app.

The app, and this site, are on GitHub.
