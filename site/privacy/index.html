Privacy Policy

Season42 for iPhone. Last updated 13 September 2026.

Season42 keeps a record of the series and movies you watch. Everything it knows is yours: you type it in, and it stays on your phone. There is no account to make, nothing to sign in to, and nothing that tracks you.

This policy describes the whole of what the app does with information, because there is not much of it to describe.

What stays on your device

Your library — every series and movie you track, their titles, descriptions, statuses, positions, dates, the streaming services you have registered, and the posters and logos you have adopted — is stored on your iPhone and nowhere else. It is never uploaded, never synchronised, and never sent to us or to anyone else. If you delete the app, that library goes with it; there is no copy anywhere for us to keep or for you to recover.

Your library may be included in your own iPhone backup, whether that backup is on your computer or in your iCloud account. That backup is Apple's and yours, and is governed by Apple's terms rather than this policy. Nothing in Season42 puts it there — it is the ordinary behaviour of an app's storage on iOS.

The one thing that leaves your device

Searching is the only part of the app that uses the network. When you search for a series, a movie, or a streaming service's logo, the text you typed is sent to a small server run for this app at season42-bff.fly.dev, which asks TMDB on your behalf and passes the answer back. If you then open a result, the app asks the same server for that title's details and its poster, by the identifier the search result carried.

Adopting a streaming service's logo works the same way: the search returns the names of the services that matched, and the picture itself is a second request for those bytes, made when you pick one.

That is the entire list. Nothing else you do in the app — opening your library, editing an entry, marking an episode watched, changing a setting — touches the network at all. The app works with no connection; only searching fails.

What the server keeps

The server holds no personal information. It stores no library, no account, and nothing tied to you or your device.

  • Search text is passed straight to TMDB and is not written down — with one exception: if TMDB cannot be reached, the failure is logged together with the text that was being searched for, so the fault can be diagnosed. A failure to fetch a title's details, its poster or a logo logs the identifier that was asked for rather than any text you typed. Successful requests are not logged at all.
  • Posters and logos fetched from TMDB are kept on the server so the next person asking for the same picture does not cause a second fetch. These are TMDB's images of films and services, and say nothing about who asked for them.
  • Your IP address is visible to the server while it answers, as it is to any server you connect to, and is used in memory to count requests against a rate limit that keeps one caller from using up the whole service. It is not stored, and it is not written into a log.

Nothing the server keeps could be used to identify you, and nothing it keeps is shared with anyone.

Third parties

TMDB (The Movie Database) is the source of all series and movie information the app can search, and of the posters and streaming service logos it offers you. Your search text reaches TMDB through the server described above. TMDB's own privacy policy governs what TMDB does with the requests it receives. This product uses TMDB and the TMDB APIs but is not endorsed, certified, or otherwise approved by TMDB.

The server the app talks to runs on Fly.io, which hosts it and routes the connection, as any host does.

There are no other third parties. Season42 contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no crash reporting, and no third-party SDKs of any kind. It does not use the advertising identifier, does not fingerprint your device, and collects nothing for marketing.

Children

Season42 collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Your choices

Because nothing about you is held anywhere but your own phone, there is nothing to request, export or have deleted on your behalf. Deleting the app deletes everything it holds. If you would rather nothing at all left your device, do not use search; the rest of the app does not need it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date above. The history of every change is public in the app's repository.
