Support

Season42 for iPhone.

Report a problem

Everything about this app is public, and so is its problem list. To report a bug or ask for something, open an issue:

Open an issue on GitHub — a GitHub account is needed, and it is free.

If you would rather not use GitHub, email season42app@outlook.com.

What helps most in a report: what you were doing, what you expected, what happened instead, and which iPhone and iOS version you are on. A screenshot is worth several paragraphs.

What support is, honestly

Season42 is written and maintained by one person in their own time, and it is free. There is no support desk, no rota and no promised response time. Reports are read, and the ones that are clear enough to act on are the ones that get fixed. Some will be answered with "that is how it works" or "not going to build that", and those answers will at least be given rather than left hanging.

If the app is not doing what you need, there are better-staffed trackers and no hard feelings.

Things worth knowing before reporting

  • Your library is on your phone and nowhere else. It is not in an account, so it cannot be recovered by signing in somewhere. Deleting the app deletes it.
  • Searching is the only thing that needs a network. If search fails and everything else works, the likely cause is the connection or the small server behind search. The rest of the app is expected to work offline.
  • Series and movie information comes from TMDB. If a title is missing, wrong, or has the wrong poster, that is TMDB's record, and correcting it there fixes it here. Anything you have copied into your library is yours and editable — the app never links back.
